🌍 COVID-19 Pandemic

Read the passage carefully

In March 2020, the World Health Organization declared COVID-19 a pandemic. The virus spread across the world, affecting millions of people. Schools closed, and many people stayed at home. People were advised to wear masks, wash their hands and maintain social distance. On 8 December 2020, the first COVID-19 vaccine was administered in the United Kingdom.
📖 Read the passage and answer your team's questions!

🟣 Team A – Skimming

Focus on the main idea and general meaning. Do not read every word.

1 What is the passage mainly about?
✅ Answer: The COVID-19 pandemic.
2 What safety measures did people follow?
✅ Answer: Wearing masks, washing hands and maintaining social distance.

🔵 Team B – Scanning

Search for specific information, dates and details.

1 In which month and year was COVID-19 declared a pandemic?
✅ Answer: March 2020.
2 On which date was the first vaccine administered in the UK?
✅ Answer: 8 December 2020.
